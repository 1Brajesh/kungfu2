KungFu 2 · look test

Everything here is drawn by code while it runs. There are no image files. Both panels play the same fight on the same skeleton with the same moves; only the drawing style differs. The poses are a rough first pass.

A · Silhouette at sunset

  • Moody and bold: every move reads from its outline alone.
  • Hides the lack of fine detail, so it looks finished sooner.
  • Colour comes from the sky, a thin rim of sunlight and the hit effects. The fighters are told apart by their ribbon colours.
  • Trade-off: less personality per fighter (no faces or outfits).

B · Clean cartoon

  • Bright and friendly. Each fighter gets their own outfit, colours, hair and face.
  • Flat colours with a hard shadow edge and dark outlines, like a modern animated show.
  • Comic hit stars and words make every hit pop.
  • Trade-off: more drawing work for each new fighter, and stiff poses show more than in silhouette.

Ideas shown in both

  • The fist or foot is the hit. Turn on Show skeleton to see the joints and the exact hit points (red).
  • Hit-freeze: the action stops for a split second on contact. The heavy kick also shakes and zooms the camera.
  • Motion streaks behind fast punches and kicks.
  • Headband ribbons with simple physics that trail and flutter behind each fighter.
  • Living backgrounds: swaying bamboo, drifting petals, birds, clouds and lanterns.
  • Slow motion shows how the poses blend into one another. Keys: Space pause, S slow, K skeleton.
